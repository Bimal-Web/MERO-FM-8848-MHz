mero-fm is a community-driven music streaming platform where listeners can enjoy the rich diversity of Nepali music. From traditional folk tunes to modern classics, it brings together generations of sound in one place.

📌 Features
Nepali Lok Bhaka / Lok Dori – Authentic folk and cultural songs from across Nepal.

Nepali Movie Songs – Timeless tracks from popular Nepali cinema.

Pop 90’s Songs – Nostalgic hits from Nepal’s golden era of pop music.

Classical Songs – Soulful and traditional compositions that preserve heritage.

🎯 Mission
To preserve and promote Nepali musical heritage while making it accessible to everyone, everywhere.
